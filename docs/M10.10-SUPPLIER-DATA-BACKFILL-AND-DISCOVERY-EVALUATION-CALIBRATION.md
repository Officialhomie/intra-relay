## 3. CandidateEvaluation calibration

`CandidateEvaluation` is additive and trace-only. `CandidateAssessment` and
`planCandidates` still exclusively determine `toQuery` and `skipped`.
Evaluation runs after capability retrieval, records a compact trace entry, and
emits `EVALUATION_POLICY_DISAGREEMENT` when its advisory conclusion would
exclude a candidate that the current policy would query.

The tests deliberately exercise every result rather than represent a sampled
production rate:

| Constraint | MATCH evidence                        | NO_MATCH evidence                | UNKNOWN evidence                         | Calibration                                                       |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fulfilment | declared pickup/delivery `true`       | declared requested flag `false`  | absent/null flag                         | Boolean result is deterministic once supplied                     |
| Location   | conservative text overlap             | never emitted                    | absent area or non-overlapping free text | Non-overlap remains UNKNOWN without a gazetteer                   |
| Turnaround | parsed typical duration fits deadline | parsed duration exceeds deadline | absent or unparsable value               | Typical duration is evidence only; quote turnaround remains final |

The M10.9 integration cases produce one fully `ELIGIBLE/HIGH` candidate, an
advisory `EXCLUDED/HIGH` case for explicit delivery=false, explicit turnaround
MATCH/NO_MATCH/UNKNOWN cases, and a multi-UNKNOWN `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION/MEDIUM`
case. The explicit delivery=false case disagrees with CandidateAssessment:
current policy remains queryable and the trace records that disagreement. A
stale candidate is excluded by both systems. There are no persisted historical
agent traces in the audited local data, so a real-run percentage cannot be
claimed.

## 5. Product type, minimum order, and buyer preference

**Product type.** M10.6 stores a canonical 14-value printing product vocabulary
as optional `productType` options on each supplier route's `inputSchema`. The
buyer-side `flyerPrintingInputSchema` accepts it, but the intent parser,
conversation flow, `BuyerIntent`, and CandidateEvaluation do not capture or
compare it. The smallest next implementation is: add an optional product-type
field to buyer intent/brief parsing, pass it into discovery criteria, and return
MATCH/NO_MATCH/UNKNOWN only when a route has declared options. No route/table
change is needed.

**Minimum order.** Tally asks for “Minimum order, and what changes the price?”
only as each service's free-text pricing note. It is not a usable numeric,
typed minimum and cannot safely drive matching. The smallest Tally change is a
separate optional/required-as-appropriate numeric “minimum order quantity”
question per selected service, normalized and persisted as a structured
per-product value. Do not attempt a route-wide minimum from prose.

**Buyer preference.** `UserIntent.optimization` is extracted, preserved by the
conversation layer, returned to the UI, and sent to analytics, but it does not
reach agent `BuyerIntent` or `scoreOffer`. The smallest change is to add the
preference to `BuyerIntent`, carry it from the launched conversation request,
and make one tested scoring/tie-break policy consume it. This is not trivially
isolated because the current launch/run boundary does not pass the conversation
intent into the agent; it is not implemented here.

## Final decision and next milestone

No new constraint is promoted automatically. Service, availability,
verification/readiness, price freshness, and payment policy retain their
existing gates. Fulfilment is the leading promotion candidate, but must first
have the approved safe backfill applied and enough real completed supplier
onboarding records to show low UNKNOWN and no unsafe disagreement pattern.
Location and turnaround must remain advisory; UNKNOWN must continue to request
confirmation rather than exclude.

**Next milestone:** apply the single reviewed backfill only after explicit
approval, instrument/export real candidate-evaluation trace aggregates by
constraint, then calibrate a fulfilment-only authoritative gate against that
evidence. Do not gate location, turnaround, product type, or minimum order in
that milestone.
