# Social template recommendations

The packaged four-format system is enough to begin publishing. It supports ten
content types through copy and evidence choices rather than ten unrelated
visual designs.

## Use now

- Profile assets for X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp.
- Minimal X and LinkedIn covers.
- Static Open Graph card.
- Square, landscape, portrait, and story SVG masters after placeholder copy is
  replaced and the PNG is re-exported.

## Content hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: content type, date, or audience.
2. Headline: one complete, concrete thought, preferably two lines or fewer.
3. Supporting detail: evidence, limitation, availability, or next action.
4. Footer: live-text Intra name; no tagline lockup.

## How the template types differ

- Announcement, release, and event posts lead with a date/state eyebrow.
- Educational and founder posts lead with a specific observation.
- Case study and trust/proof posts require a real source and a precise scope.
- Quote posts require permission and attribution; no fabricated testimonials.
- Calls to action name the next action and its consequence.

## Wait for design work

- Do not introduce illustration-led templates until the trust illustration set
  exists and has been approved as one family.
- Do not create a photography treatment until real, consented merchant or event
  photography exists.
- Do not add partner-logo bands until official assets and usage rights are
  documented.
- Do not produce a deck system by stretching social post masters; presentations
  need their own information and accessibility decisions.
