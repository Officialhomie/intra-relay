# Pre-production social system audit

Audit date: 15 September 2026. This records the package state reviewed before
the production-system expansion.

## Exists and production ready

- Canonical Handoff mark geometry and small-size favicon adaptation.
- Ink, ivory, monochrome, and reversed logo treatments.
- Transparent mark PNGs, favicons, Apple touch icon, and PWA icon exports.
- Authoritative color, type, icon, voice, and product-copy documentation.
- Static Open Graph SVG and PNG.

## Exists and needed refinement

- Social profiles existed for X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp, but lacked
  one shared optical master, a visible crop check, Facebook, and YouTube.
- X and LinkedIn covers were centered-mark placeholders rather than useful brand
  compositions and lacked coherent positioning variants.
- Four social ratio templates repeated one rounded-box composition and did not
  provide enough editorial variation for real content.
- Platform documentation did not cover Facebook, YouTube, or the complete set
  of safe-area and export rules.

## Missing

- YouTube and Facebook systems; Instagram reel-specific cover; full LinkedIn
  company assets; master profile variants; ten content families; screenshot
  frames; diagrams; presentation layouts; safe-area guides; contact sheets;
  partner handling; and a final delivery index.
- Real approved product screenshots, official partner marks, a canonical vector
  wordmark, and a coherent illustration set.

## Do not create

- A replacement identity, second symbol, mascot, fake vector wordmark, or new palette.
- Unofficial partner marks, fabricated screenshots, merchants, prices, outcomes,
  testimonials, or customer photography.
- Generic AI gradients, crypto motifs, glowing effects, 3D blobs, or stock imagery.

## Duplicate and naming findings

The original package contained purposeful platform aliases of the same source
geometry. The expanded system keeps recognizable platform filenames while
making the four ratio masters and profile master the reusable sources. Legacy
alias filenames remain for compatibility and are documented in the exact tree.
