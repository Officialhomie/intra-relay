# Partner logo handling

1. Source marks only from the rights holder's official brand kit.
2. Record the source URL, retrieval date, license, and any approval requirement.
3. Keep partner files in a clearly named future `brand/partners/<partner>/` folder.
4. Preserve required clear space, colors, minimum size, and lockups.
5. Never redraw, recolor, outline, crop, or imply endorsement.
6. Keep partner marks separate from prices, guarantees, attestations, and trust claims.
7. Use text-only partner references until approved artwork is available.

## Lockup convention

- Separate the Intra mark and partner mark with a thin warm-border divider.
- Give each mark clear space equal to its own published minimum, using the
  larger requirement when the two differ.
- Match optical height rather than forcing identical bounding-box dimensions.
- Use full-color marks on ivory when both guides permit it.
- Use each owner's supplied monochrome artwork on a single-color campaign; do
  not create a monochrome conversion from full-color artwork.
- Put “In collaboration with”, “Works with”, or another approved relationship
  label above the lockup. Never let spacing alone imply the relationship.

## Attribution convention

Place legal or trademark attribution in small sans text below the campaign
content, outside the primary message. Copy the owner-provided wording exactly
and keep source and approval records with the campaign files.

The current package intentionally includes no unofficial partner marks.
